Setup tips

  1. Log in to Notion first (the free plan works), then click Duplicate at the top right of the template and choose your workspace.
  2. Delete the sample rows, or keep them as examples.
  3. Set your tax set-aside rate (default 25%) in Tax Set-Aside and Invoices.
  4. Change the currency: click a money property → Edit property → Number format.
  5. Add your first client, then a project, then an invoice.
  6. Each month, add a Tax Set-Aside row (YYYY-MM), link that month's paid invoices and expenses, and tick “Moved” once you've transferred the money.
